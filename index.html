About

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. researcher at the University of New Mexico working on trustworthy autonomous systems at the intersection of robotics, formal verification, reinforcement learning, and control. My research focuses on developing models, verification methods, and planning/control techniques that provide stronger safety and correctness guarantees for autonomous and cyber-physical systems.

My background spans ROS/ROS 2, distributed robotic systems, stochastic control, reinforcement learning, formal methods, and production software engineering. I also have prior industry experience in robot teleoperation, cloud-connected mobile robotics, backend systems, and secure enterprise software.

Research Interests

Formal Verification for Robotics

Model checking, temporal properties, abstraction, reachability, and verification of robotic software and communication systems.

Safe & Trustworthy Autonomy

Safety-oriented planning and control for autonomous and cyber-physical systems under uncertainty.

Reinforcement Learning & Control

Safe RL, model-based and model-free control, stochastic optimal control, robustness, and performance guarantees.

ROS 2 & Distributed Robotic Systems

Publisher-subscriber semantics, DDS behavior, runtime tracing, queueing, nondeterminism, and communication correctness.

Current Research Projects

Safe Path Planning for N-CAV

Developing safety-aware planning and abstraction methods for multiple connected and autonomous vehicles using feasible trajectories, backward reachability, partitioning, and compositional reasoning.

Formal Verification of ROS 2 Communication

Modeling ROS 2 publisher-subscriber behavior, including timers, bounded queues, message loss, callback execution, and nondeterministic scheduling, with formal verification of safety and progress properties.

VeriSim

Research on verifiable modeling workflows for neural-network-controlled switched systems, connecting simulation models with formal representations and controller artifacts.

Stochastic Control & Safe Learning

Studying stochastic optimal control, abstraction-based methods, reinforcement learning, robustness under uncertainty, and scalable approaches to safety guarantees.
